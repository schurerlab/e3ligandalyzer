    About This Integration

    The E3 Ligase Expression Atlas module streamlines a curated subset of data from the ELiAH database, originally published in Database (Oxford), 2024 . It focuses exclusively on tissue-resolved expression patterns of E3 ubiquitin ligases across human organs.

    Each ligase’s mean Z-score is computed per tissue from normalized transcript data, allowing fast comparison of expression abundance and tissue selectivity. This streamlined dataset underpins the E3 Ligase Atlas Explorer visualization tool, linking tissue context to structural and functional ligand data.

    We gratefully acknowledge the original authors of the ELiAH resource — Hyojung Paik, Chunryong Oh, Sajid Hussain, Sangjae Seo, Soon Woo Park, Tae Lyun Ko, and Ari Lee.

    If you use this integration in your research or applications, please cite their publication: “ELiAH: the atlas of E3 ligases in human tissues for targeted protein degradation with reduced off-target effect”, DOI: 10.1093/database/baae111 .
