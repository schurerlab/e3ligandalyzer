What to report

Useful reports include peptide-like fragments that slipped through filtering, BRD or BIRD-like molecules that appear incompletely represented, single-ligand assignments that look suspicious, partial ligand extraction, missing PDB or SDF files, missing SASA or visualization assets, incorrect recruiter codes, duplicate records, incorrect ligase or scaffold assignments, broken downloads, broken API links, or misleading external references.

  • Peptide-like fragments or incompletely extracted molecules.
  • BRD/BIRD-like molecules that appear truncated or only partly represented.
  • Missing PDB, SDF, SASA, SVG, 2D, or 3D assets.
  • Incorrect recruiter code, ligase, scaffold, residue, chain, or PDB mapping.
  • Duplicate or suspicious recruiter records and broken download or API routes.
The generated message is sent through your own email client to {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. The authors may follow up if additional evidence is needed, and confirmed corrections can be incorporated into future curated releases.
