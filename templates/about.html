Dataset context

How to interpret the counts

The Ligandalyzer distinguishes between unique recruiter ligands and curated structural instances, because the same ligand can appear in more than one structural context.

In V1, 345 reconciled recruiter entities are represented across 820 observed structure instances. One entity can be observed in multiple ligase, PDB, chain, residue, or variant contexts.

Our mission

Bring order to recruiter discovery.

The E3 Ligase Ligandalyzer was built to bring order and insight to one of the most rapidly evolving areas of modern drug discovery — targeted protein degradation. This platform provides a living, interactive atlas of E3 ligase complexes, recruiter scaffolds, and ligand structures.

From cryo-EM and crystal structures to virtual docking predictions, every data point contributes to a collective understanding of how molecular glues and PROTACs engage the ubiquitin system.

What began as an internal visualization utility has grown into a full-fledged research companion — uniting cheminformatics, bioinformatics, and structural biology under one interactive interface.

The vision ahead

An open ecosystem for rational degrader design.

Our long-term goal is to establish an open-access ecosystem for degrader science — where researchers can not only visualize and compare existing structures, but also design new linkers, rank ternary poses, and analyze interaction fingerprints in real time.

AI-assisted ligand ranking

Using interaction density and docking metrics to prioritize candidates.

Automated linker generation

Connecting recruiter–target pairs through design-aware linkers.

Cross-ligase scaffold comparison

Identifying shared pharmacophores and transferable recruiter logic.

Experimental + user data integration

Creating a living hub for community-driven degrader discovery.

Together, these tools will transform the Ligandalyzer into a collaborative hub for rational degrader discovery.
