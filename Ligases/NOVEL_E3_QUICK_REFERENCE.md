## 🔬 WHY THESE LIGASES MATTER

### HECT Ligases (NEW CLASS!)
- **No HECT ligases in current dataset** → First-in-class PROTAC recruiters
- Allosteric binding sites → ideal exit vectors for PROTAC linkers
- Validated druggability (SMURF1/2, WWP1, NEDD4, UBR5)
- Broad disease relevance: cancer, fibrosis, neurodegeneration

### RBR Ligases (NOVEL MECHANISM!)
- Hybrid RING-HECT architecture → distinct from classical E3s
- E3-E3 cooperativity with CRLs (ARIH1/2) → amplified ubiquitination
- Linear ubiquitin chains (HOIP) → unique NF-κB regulation
- Parkin precedent validates RBR druggability

### F-Box Proteins (MOLECULAR GLUE!)
- WD40 domains → CRBN-like molecular glue potential
- Substrate recognition modulators → alternative to enzyme inhibition
- FBXW7: most mutated E3 in cancer (c-Myc, Notch, cyclin E degradation)
- β-TrCP1: master regulator (β-catenin, IκBα degradation)
