## 🔬 METHODOLOGY

### Evidence Scoring (0-10):
- PDB Structures: 0-3 points (scaled by count/10)
- SDF Ligands: 0-2 points (scaled by count/20)
- Crystallography: +2 points
- Sequence Data: +1 point
- Database Recruiters: +2 points

### Readiness Classification:
- **CLINICAL:** Validated PROTACs in development (CRBN, VHL)
- **PRECLINICAL:** Evidence score ≥7, extensive structural data
- **RESEARCH:** Evidence score 4-6, moderate structural coverage
- **EXPLORATORY:** Evidence score 2-3, limited data
- **NASCENT:** Evidence score <2, minimal characterization

### PROTAC Feasibility Score (0-20):
- Base readiness score (2-10)
- Structural bonus (+1 to +3)
- Ligand library bonus (+1 to +3)
- Crystallography bonus (+2)
- Gap penalty (-0.5 × gap_score)
